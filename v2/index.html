Questions, answered.

Do I actually need a Level 5 finish?

Usually for big open rooms with strong window light or gloss paint, where any lesser finish shows every joint. We’ll tell you honestly whether your walls need it or whether a standard Level 4 will look just as good.

Can you match the texture on my walls?

Yes. We match orange peel, knockdown and skip trowel, including older hand-applied textures nobody stocks a recipe for. We sample the wall and blend past the patch line so it doesn’t read as a repair.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Next Gen Drywall LLC holds Arizona ROC license 334040 (CR-10 Drywall) and carries general liability and workers’ compensation. The license is statewide for commercial and residential work.

Do you install fire-rated drywall?

Yes. We install fire-rated assemblies that meet code and project specifications on commercial and multifamily work.

Can you work in a home we’re living in?

Yes. We work efficiently in occupied homes, keep disruption to a minimum and keep the site clean.

How do builders get a bid?

Send plans through the bid form. No charge, no obligation, and you get a clear, itemized quote. For production builders we’re happy to set up a standing bid relationship so you’re not starting from scratch each phase.
