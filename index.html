The board · What goes into a drywall bid

Every wall is different.
Here’s what sets
your number.

Drywall pricing swings with the job, so we don’t post a menu. These are the eight things we measure on the walkthrough, and every one of them shows up as its own line on your itemized bid.

01Square footage

Sq ft

On your bid: Every wall and ceiling measured from your plans or on the walkthrough. The biggest line on any bid.

02Ceiling height

Height

On your bid: Standard 8-foot rooms move fastest. Vaulted great rooms and stairwells need stilts, lifts or scaffold.

03Finish level

L3–L5

On your bid: Each level adds coats and sanding. Level 5 is a full skim, the right call for big rooms in low evening light.

04Texture

Match

On your bid: New smooth walls, or matching the orange peel, knockdown or skip trowel you already have so repairs disappear.

05Board type

Type X

On your bid: Standard, moisture-resistant or fire-rated board, set by code and by the room it goes in.

06Occupied home

Lived-in

On your bid: Floors covered, furniture moved, dust kept down in a home you’re living in. Empty new builds go faster.

07Schedule

Dates

On your bid: One room or a phased 200-unit build. Crew size follows the dates, and the start date we give is the one that holds.

08Repairs

Damage

On your bid: How big the hole is, whether water got behind it, and how far the texture has to blend past the patch.
